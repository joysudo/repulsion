# Oh My Gatos...

_Oh My Gatos_ is a cooperative 2-player platformer built in 48 hours. Inspired by classic Fireboy/Watergirl mechanics, you play as two magnetic cats who must use their polarities to navigate puzzles, attract each other across gaps, and reach the end of each level safely.

_Oh My Gatos_ is built in Godot 4.5 by the same team who brought you **[Cattatime](https://github.com/joysudo/cattatime)**: [Joy Su](https://github.com/joysudo), [Kat Wang](https://github.com/ikealoverkat), and [Kaylee Dinh](https://github.com/kaylz-d).
